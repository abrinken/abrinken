WE'VE OCCUPIED ⒶBRINKEN

We're a group of friendly anarchists who've occupied a row of largely abandoned allotment gardens by Brabrandstien. We have named the area Ⓐbrinken. Here, we will build the new world in the shell of the old; a living, diverse refuge, harboring space for nature and the whole of human diversity; a local community built on solidarity, mutual aid, and equality, free from the suppression and alienation we see and experience each and every day, in both the society around us & the rest of the world.

THE AREA

Ⓐbrinken is part of a larger area of scenic nature & garden houses by Brabrandstien in Aarhus C. DSB Ejendomme believes they have the right to the area themselves, and as such, they've terminated the leases of the area's inhabitants, without giving any form of compensation. The tenants were terminated due to a larger construction project meant to be built on-site by DSB in cooperation with the property development group 1927 Estate. This had to be dropped, however, when it was uncovered that the Head of Construction had been illegally dumping 10,000 tons of highly contaminated soil, as documented in the tv-program "Den Sorte Svane" (The Black Swan).

Despite the changes to the plans, DSB insists that the inhabitants must be evicted, now under the excuse of wanting to build some sort of park in the area. The description of the plan is however so vague and incomplete that taking it seriously comes with great difficulty.

CALL TO ACTION

We stand in solidarity with the people living here; why are they being evicted after living here for 30 years? Why is it that, time and time again, big corporations get to dictate how our city is shaped? Let's stand together and speak out against this suppression and together build the world we want to see.

It's time to get organized and claim our right to the city; claim our right to self determination and local autonomy; claim our and everybody's right to a good and fullfilling life here on this earth.

Free-spaces & sanctuaries such as this don't just appear of their own accord — they're something we must work and struggle for. Take part in the struggle, so that we may stand stronger when the state & DSB will attempt to suppress and remove us. We need your awesome powers and wicked ideas. Together, let's write history!
