Muliggør Ⓐbrinken! 🏡 Demonstration søndag den 1. November 📣

📍Vi mødes ved Institut for X 🗓️ 12:00 Søndag den 1. Nov.

(English below)

Vi går på gaden for at råbe politikerne og de ansvarlige byrådsmedlemmer op.

Hvorfor skulle Ⓐbrinken jævnes med jorden?

Hvorfor nægtede kommunen dialog? Hvorfor valgte de i stedet af bruge millioner af skattekroner og tusinder af arbejdstimer på at udslette et fredeligt kulturelt og socialt fællesskab, der blomstrede midt i Aarhus?

Hvem var det, der tog den beslutning?

De ansvarlige politikere gjorde 8 udsatte unge hjemløse med deres dyre magtdemonstration. De smadrede naturen og fristedet. De rev os ud af sengene, stadig i undertøj. De forvoldte stor skade både fysisk og psykisk, som hele fælleskabet stadig lider under i dag. Alt dette for hvad?

Hvordan er det et demokrati, hvis der ikke er plads til dialog?

Kom med på gaden, den 1. November. Hjælp os med at få politikerne i dialog. Lad os sammen Muliggøre Ⓐbrinken!

English:

Make Ⓐbrinken possible! Demonstration Sunday the 1st of November!

📍Institut for X 🗓️ 12:00 Sunday Nov 1st.

We're marching on the street to call out politicians and responsible city council members.

Why was Abrinken razed to the ground?

Why did the municipality reject having a dialogue? Why did they spend taxpayer millions as well as thousands of worker hours on destroying a peaceful social and cultural community blooming in the middle of Aarhus?

Who made that decision?

The responsible politicians made 8 at-risk youths homeless with their expensive power demonstration. They destroyed nature and the sanctuary. They ripped us out of our beds in our underwear. They caused incredible harm, both physically and psychologically, which the entire community is still suffering from. All of this for what?

What kind of democracy is this with no space for dialogue?

Join us on the street on the 1st of November. Help us demand a dialogue from the politicians. Together, let's make Ⓐbrinken possible!
