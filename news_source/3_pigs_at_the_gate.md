title: Pigs at the gate
published: 2026-08-27

Call to action! 

Fellow activists, we have now had our first real interaction with the police. Four of them showed up on our doorstep last night, with another eight (plus some dogs) arriving soon after to guard the perimeter. 

We got enough advance warning from our perimeter guard to be able to send out a greeting party. The interaction proceeded amicably enough, despite the tense situation. The cops took a bunch of photos, tried (and failed) to get information from us, and went on their way. 

What became clear to us is that we do not currently have enough people on site to deal with an actual eviction attempt. We would like to change that. 

So if you’ve been here before and know you want to return, or if you’ve been thinking about dropping by to help make this place a permanent reality, we urge you to consider now the time to do so. 

Let us gather our strength and come together now. Let’s make sure that Ⓐbrinken is here to stay!
